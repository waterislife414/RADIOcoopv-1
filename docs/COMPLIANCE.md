# Compliance checklist (manager-owned; workers never edit)
Source: Ala. Code 35-12-93 as amended by HB104 (2026RS, eff. 2026-06-01). Re-verify before first contact; the governor's signature on HB104 was not confirmed.
- Waiting period: an agreement signed from presumption of abandonment until 24 months after the property is paid or delivered to the State Treasurer is void and unenforceable. Exception: attorneys filing a claim for identified property.
- Fee cap: total compensation <= 10% of value claimed. No buying or assigning the property to us if our net profit would exceed 10%.
- Agreement must: be written; describe the property and our services; state the property is in the custody of the State Treasurer's Office with recovery contact info; state the owner can recover it from the Treasurer WITHOUT a fee; be signed by the apparent owner; state our fee percentage; list any other compensation.
- Claim filing: give the Treasurer a full unredacted copy of the signed agreement. The Treasurer may ask for more proof of ownership.
- No state finder license, registration or bond in Alabama (per the bill text). Other states: verify each before touching them. Georgia requires one: do not work Georgia properties.
- Readiness check: An owner is ready for outreach only if they pass all checks in `uprec.readiness.outreach_blockers()`.
## Practice rules
- Letters by first-class mail first. No automated texts or robocalls without prior written consent (TCPA).
- Say who we are, never imply we are the state. Always state the free claim option up front.
- Heirs: claimant must prove entitlement (death certificate, relationship, estate documents). Get the Treasurer's current requirements before drafting heir packets.
- The Treasurer pays the claimant, not us. Agreement must say when and how the fee is paid after the owner is paid.
- Payments, crypto valuation and mineral-rights compensation follow the statute text; never share underlying minerals.
- Privacy: owner data stays in data/ (gitignored). Free AI tiers can log prompts, so no owner PII is ever sent to a worker model.
- Not legal advice; consider one attorney review of the agreement and letter templates before sending.
