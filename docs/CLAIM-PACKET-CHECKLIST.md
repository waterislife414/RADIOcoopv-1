# Claim packet checklist (manager-owned)
Get the Treasurer's CURRENT claim form and requirements from the official site before each filing. Requirements change; nothing below replaces them.
## Always
- [ ] Signed finder agreement, filed unredacted with the claim (signed on or after the eligible_after date)
- [ ] The Treasurer's claim form, completed and signed by the owner
- [ ] Copy of the owner's government photo ID
- [ ] Proof of the address history if the property address differs from the current one
- [ ] Property ID(s) from the portal record
- [ ] Our record of who we contacted, when, and how (outreach table)
## Heir or beneficiary claims (add)
- [ ] Death certificate
- [ ] Proof of relationship to the deceased
- [ ] Estate documents if the Treasurer requires them (letters of administration, small estate affidavit, will)
- [ ] Heir's ID and signed claim
## Business claims (add)
- [ ] Secretary of State record showing the entity and who may sign for it
- [ ] Authorization of the signer (officer title or resolution)
- [ ] Dissolved entity: documents showing who the owners/shareholders are
## After filing
- [ ] Log filing date in agreements.claim_filed_on, follow up with the Treasurer
- [ ] When the owner is paid, log claim_paid_on, send the fee invoice per the agreement
